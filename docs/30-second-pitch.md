# FlowGuard RecoveryPilot — 30-Second Pitch

Payment degradation can quietly put merchant value at risk, but a fixed retry
does not know which opportunity is worth an intervention. FlowGuard detects an
emerging UPI Intent degradation, estimates recovery probability and expected
simulated value, and ranks the opportunity for a merchant-approved bounded
payment attempt. Deterministic policy, SHA-256 idempotency and payment
verification ensure that only a verified paid outcome becomes recovered value.
Our M6 result is reproducible synthetic evidence across 20 seeds—not a claim
about production Razorpay revenue.
